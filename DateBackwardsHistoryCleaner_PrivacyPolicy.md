# Privacy Policy

**Date-Backwards History Cleaner** is a Chrome extension that helps you remove older browsing-history entries and download-list records. The extension does not send your browsing data or settings to the developer or to an analytics service.

**Information accessed and why.** When you request an estimate or start a purge, the extension uses Chrome's History and Downloads APIs to identify records before your chosen cutoff and apply your protected-domain list. If you enable automatic purging, it accesses the selected records when the scheduled purge runs. It accesses download-list details only; downloaded files and their contents are never read, moved, or deleted. Recent history may also be checked so that visits to pages you visited recently can be preserved when protected domains are configured.

**Information stored.** Settings, retention schedules, protected domains, and purge activity logs are stored in Chrome's local extension storage on your device. Logs contain run times, cutoff, trigger, selected data types, counts, status, and any error message; they do not contain the URLs visited or downloaded. Data you export is saved by your browser as a file at your request.

**Sharing and external services.** The extension does not upload, sell, or share the information it accesses. It makes no analytics or advertising network requests and loads no remote code. Optional support or sponsor links, if enabled, open external websites only when you choose to click them; those sites operate under their own privacy policies.

**Retention and controls.** Stored settings and protected domains remain in the extension until you change or remove them or uninstall the extension. The activity log keeps at most the 500 most recent purge records; you can clear it from Settings. You can disable automatic purging in Settings. Exported files are under your control and must be deleted separately if no longer wanted.

**Permissions.** The `history` and `downloads` permissions are used only to estimate and remove the record types you select. `storage` saves your settings and logs locally, and `alarms` runs automatic purges you configure.
